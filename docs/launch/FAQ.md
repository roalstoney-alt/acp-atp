# FAQ

## Is PATL an AI assistant?

No. PATL is a trust and enforcement layer for personal agents.

## Does PATL connect to my email or payment accounts?

No. v0.1 alpha uses synthetic data and mock services only.

## Can Agent credit override a blocked action?

No. Credit cannot override permission.

## Are critical decisions made by an LLM?

No. Critical alpha controls are deterministic.

## Is this production-ready?

No. PATL v0.1 is an alpha for review and validation.
